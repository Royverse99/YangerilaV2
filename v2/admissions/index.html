Rules regarding Batch hub and fee submission

  • Batch hub is a center place for all academy related resources including – Zoom Meeting link, Course sheets, Notices and more.
  • Batch hub can be accessed only by students enrolled in the academy at any time. New students will be able to access the batch hub upon admission.
  • All online classes will be accessible using the batch hub. Students will be provided the join button and meeting link in the batch hub.
  • Zoom meeting links will be renewed on the 7th of each month and will be updated only in the batch hub. Meeting link will not be provided over WhatsApp or any other medium.
  • Fees should be paid by the 7th of each month. Failing which the access to the batch hub will be revoked until the fee is paid. This will be an automated process and not done manually.
  • Any classes missed after 7th due to non payment of fees will be counted as lapsed and no back up class will be provided for these classes.
  • A late fee charge of 250/- INR will be applicable if the fee is still unpaid after 10th of the running month.
  • In case a student is unable to pay the fee in a given month, they should contact the academy for the option of delayed payments.

Rules regarding classes missed / backup classes

  • No backup class will be provided for uninformed absence. Academy must be notified for absence, via message on group or private chat, at least 6 hours prior to commencement of the class. Emergency situations exempted.
  • Any class missed should be covered in the form of a backup class by the student within 14 days from the date the class was held. Failing which the backup class will be lapsed.
  • No backup classes will be provided in the next month until the next month’s payment is received. Any student who wishes to quit the classes after the running month should inform the academy beforehand.
  • It is the sole responsibility of the student/guardian to contact the academy for arranging a backup class. Academy will not call for arranging backup classes.
  • In case of long absence (holiday/business trip/exams), a student must notify the academy in advance and pre-schedule classes that will be missed.

Rules regarding Class Breaks and Rejoining

  • To ensure steady progress and maintain the flow of learning, we encourage students to attend classes regularly. Taking a break of one or two months disrupts the course structure and creates an uneven pace within the batch.
  • If a student decides to take a break, rejoining will be treated as a new admission. This means:
    • An admission fee will be charged again upon rejoining.
    • The applicable fee structure at the time of rejoining will be followed, even if there has been a fee revision.
    • As of 1st, January 2025, Monthly fee is 3200/- and 800/- admission fee is charged upon joining.
  • To run the academy more efficiently we have decided to implement these rules for all students. Your support will help us focus more on creativity and education and serve you better.
  • Thank you!
