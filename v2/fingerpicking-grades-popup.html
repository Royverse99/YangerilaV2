Fingerpicking Courses

The soulful art of fingerstyle guitar

Fingerpicking is one of the most soulful and captivating ways to play the guitar. Instead of just strumming, you use your fingers to weave together bass lines, chords, and melodies, creating music that feels complete even when played solo. It’s the sound you hear in unplugged Bollywood hits, soothing folk songs, or even in the styles of artists like Ed Sheeran and John Mayer. Whether it’s a quiet evening at home or performing for friends, fingerpicking lets you create music that feels rich, emotional, and deeply personal.

If you dream of holding an audience with just you and your guitar, fingerpicking is your path.

About the Course:

Yangerila’s Fingerpicking Grade 1 is designed to help students explore one of the most expressive and versatile guitar techniques. Fingerpicking allows you to play melody, harmony, and rhythm all at once, giving your music a fuller and more dynamic sound. From soft acoustic ballads to intricate patterns, this course builds the skills to perform confidently with your fingers rather than a pick.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Basic fingerpicking patterns for popular songs
  • Proper right-hand technique and finger independence
  • Arpeggios and broken chord patterns
  • Alternating bass lines with melody
  • Combining chords with fingerpicked melodies
  • Playing in different time signatures
  • Arranging songs in fingerpicking patterns
  • Introduction to percussive fingerpicking elements

Upon Completion:

After completing Fingerpicking Grade 1, students will be able to play and sing songs using fingerpicking patterns and rhythms, add melodies into chord progressions, and confidently perform acoustic pieces with expression and clarity. Fingerpicking will add a new dimension to their skillsets. They’ll also have a solid foundation to move into advanced fingerstyle and percussive techniques in Grade 2.
