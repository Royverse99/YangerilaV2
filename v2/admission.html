Step 1 — Terms & Conditions

Please review carefully. You must accept to continue.

1) Admissions & Eligibility

  • Admission is open to learners aged 6+ (younger applicants may be assessed on a case-by-case basis).
  • Placement into batches is based on availability, skill assessment, and scheduling compatibility.
  • We may request a short level-check (call/video) to place you correctly.

2) Fee Structure, Billing & Refunds

  • Fees are charged on a monthly cycle in advance and secure your seat for that month.
  • Once a batch slot is confirmed and classes allocated, fees are non-refundable.
  • Late payments may lead to suspension of access until dues are cleared.
  • Any discounts/coupons are valid only at the time of billing unless stated otherwise.

3) Scheduling, Attendance & Make-ups

  • Please attend your allotted batch on time; regularity is key to progress.
  • If you notify us at least 24 hours in advance and the teacher approves, a make-up may be offered subject to availability.
  • Teacher reschedules will be communicated; we’ll provide an alternate slot or consolidate the session.

4) Learning Materials & Intellectual Property

  • All materials including “Smart Sheets”, recordings, project files and handouts are for personal learning only.
  • Sharing, distributing, re-uploading or commercial use is not permitted without written consent.
  • We may water-mark or track materials to prevent misuse.

5) Conduct & Community Standards

  • Treat instructors and peers with respect; harassment or disruptive behavior isn’t tolerated.
  • Keep class groups uncluttered; use official channels for doubts, submissions and announcements.

6) Projects, Performances & Publicity

  • With your permission, we may showcase select performances on YCS channels (social/web) to celebrate progress.
  • You can withdraw consent for future posts by notifying us in writing.

7) Privacy & Data

  • We collect basic contact details to manage your learning journey. We do not sell your data.
  • Payment screenshots are stored privately in our Google Drive; the sheet keeps a view-only link for verification.

8) Safety & Health

  • Warm-up responsibly and inform us of any pre-existing conditions.
  • For minors, a parent/guardian should monitor home practice and device use where applicable.

9) Technology Requirements (Online/Hybrid)

  • A stable internet connection, webcam/mic, and a quiet space are recommended.
  • Keep your app/browser updated; we can share basic troubleshooting tips but can’t guarantee third-party platforms.

10) Communication & Support

  • Official updates go via WhatsApp/SMS/email from YCS numbers/emails. Please keep your info up to date.
  • SRM support hours are typically 10:00–19:00 IST on working days.

11) Accuracy of Information

  • You agree that the information submitted in this form is true and complete to the best of your knowledge.

12) Changes to Policy

Policies may be updated. The latest version on our website/form supersedes previous versions.

By clicking “Accept & Continue”, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agree to these Terms & Conditions.
